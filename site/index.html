One wall for my web real estate. Every domain I run is a cluster of coloured tiles — does the name resolve, does the page answer, is the certificate healthy, is the registration about to lapse, did the last deploy land, is CI green — and one integer sorts the wall, most urgent first. A human can override that integer, visibly.

Panopticon, inverted: garage door open, everyone watches together.
